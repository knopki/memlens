# 0005. Tree and writer

Status: accepted

## Context

The notes are the leaves, and a tree of retellings is a cache above them. The fold keeps the view under the budget. The writer runs outside the agent loop, from a queue of jobs.

## Decisions

| ID | Date | Decision | Context | Alternatives | Consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D-013 | 2026-11-04 | The memory is a log of curated notes with a tree of retellings above them. The notes are the leaves. A catalog of cards is the level above the notes. One engine builds both trees. | The customer gave the product shape. A chat history costs tokens and loses the old facts. | An infinite chat. A vector search over a document store. An entity graph. | Two levels on one engine. The catalog is per profile. The shape of the tree stays open. See [Q-021](03-open-questions.md#q-021). |
| D-018 | 2026-11-04 | The project takes these parts of the prior art. The first part: the frontier geometry and the addresses `#id` and `#a-b`. The second part: the most overdue pair rule, the retry by the length ruler, and the shrink check. The third part: the background compactor, the scope model, and the port form. The fourth part: the freeze for a session, the idempotency by `sourceId`, and `supersedes`. The project does not take a blocking wake or maintenance in the agent loop. It does not take a regular expression search, a message as a leaf, or an unbounded view. | The customer examined seven implementations and selected the parts. Each refused part has a product reason. | Adopt one implementation as a whole. Invent the design without the prior art. | The project keeps the invariants and refuses the product decisions that cost a turn or an unbounded view. The export and the import move the records between machines. The address uses `pos`, not `#id` (D-062). The key is `idem`, not `sourceId` (D-026). |
| D-019 | 2026-11-04 | The writer defaults follow the measurements. The writer effort is off, one batch holds 6 nodes, and the compactor runs up to 8 jobs in parallel. | The measurements give a large cost for a higher effort: 8.8 k output tokens and 120 s for one retelling. | A higher effort by default. One job at a time. | A cheap default. A user can raise the effort when the quality is not sufficient. D-054 gives six calls in a run. |
| D-038 | 2026-11-04 | A node has the key `(level, start)`, and a node of level `L` covers `2^L` consecutive `pos` values with `start` a multiple of `2^L`. The tree needs no dense prefix. | A hide, a redact, an accept, and a lazy build make holes, and a dense prefix would rebuild the tree at each change. | A dense prefix of the live positions. A child list without the `(level, start)` key. | A node keeps its range after an empty position. The frontier renders no empty position. An address stays stable after a hide. |
| D-039 | 2026-11-04 | A summary line targets 512 bytes and stops at 640 bytes. | A summary covers more than one note, so it needs more room than the 384-byte note body. | Use the note body limits for a summary. Set no limit for a summary. | One summary costs about 512 bytes of the slice. A line above 640 bytes is over the limit. |
| D-040 | 2026-11-04 | The frontier lists the lines from the oldest to the newest. A line holds the address, the range of dates, and the text. The last lines are the records that no node covers. | The agent reads the map from the old context to the fresh one, and a fresh record can wait for the writer. | List the fresh lines first. List the nodes without the records. | A reader finds the recent lines at the end. The count of the last lines changes with the writer. |
| D-041 | 2026-11-04 | The rebuild follows the dirty marks. A node is dirty when a source changes or becomes dirty, and an import that changes the order makes the tree dirty. The writer and the fold take the work in one order. A node waits for its children. | S-030 gives the dirty rule, and S-040 gives `due` as one order for the fold and the rebuild. | Keep a measure of overdue for the order of the work. | The work follows the changes. A node waits for its children. A merge can make the whole tree dirty. |
| D-048 | 2026-11-04 | The fold takes a pair by `due` in descending order, then `level` in ascending order, then `start` in ascending order. It works in batches from `high` down to `low`. | A batch keeps the view as a prefix extension, so the prompt cache of the model survives. | One merge for each turn. The newest pair first. | The old end of the log compresses first. A view above `high` shrinks to `low`. |
| D-054 | 2026-11-04 | The compaction runs outside the agent loop. The store keeps the queue of the jobs. A worker holds a job under a lease of 120 seconds, and a failed job waits 10, 60, then 300 seconds. A run takes one form: 6 calls of one line, or one call of 6 lines. The wake does not wait for the queue. | S-051 gives the queue and the four triggers. D-019 gave up to 8 jobs in parallel, and this statement gives 6 calls. NG-006 and NG-007 exclude a blocking wake and work in the agent loop. | Work in the agent loop. A separate service. A blocking wake. | A crash returns a job after the lease. The wake can give the view with the raw records. The check of `gen` refuses a result over a changed input. The count of the calls changes from D-019. |
| D-059 | 2026-11-04 | A merge gives the model the two children only. The form `parallel` gives its 6 calls one prefix of the context. The form `batch` writes up to 6 lines in the form `#a-b: line`, and a broken answer gives one retry with the error, then `parallel`. The form `auto` follows the family of the model. The writer makes 120 calls in one hour for one store at most. | A shared prefix costs almost nothing on the prefix cache. A broken batch costs 6 nodes at once, so the fallback to `parallel` limits the loss. | The form `parallel` only. The form `batch` only. No rate limit. | The cache keeps the parallel form cheap. A broken answer costs one more call. A call above the limit waits, and no work is lost. |
| D-067 | 2026-11-04 | The writer takes the provider of the model route when `provider` is empty. A writer run with an empty model fails when the effort is not `off`. | S-067 gives `provider: ""` and `model: ""` in the general config, and the preset sets only `model: deepseek-flash`. `ctx.llm.stream` needs a provider and a model. | An empty provider is a load error. The writer uses the agent route. | The default config runs no writer, because the effort is `off`. A preset that turns the effort on must set the model. |
